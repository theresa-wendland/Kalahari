I fell in love with collecting beautiful stones long before I started turning them into jewelry. Over the years, my collection grew—and so did my love for creating pieces from the treasures I found.

Eventually, I realized I had collected far too many beautiful stones to keep them all to myself. Kalahari is my way of sharing some of that beauty with you.
